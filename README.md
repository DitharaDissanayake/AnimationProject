2D Animated Short Story

## Project Overview

Don't Forget Your Roots is a 2D animated short story created using Adobe Animate. It presents an emotional story about a young girl who receives a banana plant from a kind woman and learns to earn money by taking care of it.

The animation highlights the importance of gratitude, responsibility, and remembering the things that helped us in life.

## Story Description

A kind woman gives a young girl a banana plant and encourages her to take care of it and earn money from its fruits.

The girl carefully looks after the plant, collects bananas, and sells them at the market. By doing this, she earns money and gradually improves her life.

After some time, she buys a mobile phone. However, she becomes addicted to using it and starts neglecting the banana plant.

Because she no longer takes proper care of it, the plant eventually dries up and stops producing bananas.

One day, while using her phone, the girl accidentally drops it and breaks it. She realizes that she needs money to repair or replace the phone. At that moment, she remembers the banana plant that once helped her earn money.

Unfortunately, the plant is already dead, and she can no longer earn anything from it.

## Main Message

**Do not forget or neglect the things that helped you succeed just because something new makes you happy.**

This story reminds us that:
- We should appreciate the help we receive from others.
- We must take responsibility for the opportunities given to us.
- Too much attachment to temporary pleasures can make us forget important responsibilities.
- We should take care of the things that support our growth and success.

## How to Open the Project

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project source `.fla` file using Adobe Animate.
3. Keep the required image and audio assets in their original relative folder structure.
4. Open the animation in Adobe Animate to explore and edit the scenes.

Note: The exported SWF files are included as project outputs. Playback support depends on the software or environment used.
